Privacy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short version

Your ideas and plans stay on your device. We don't collect them, see them or sell them.

What stays on your device

Ideas, answers, plans and edits are saved in your browser's storage on this device only. There is no account. Clearing your browser data or removing the app deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account
  • No analytics, ads or tracking
  • We don't sell or share your data

Voice input

If you use the mic button, your browser or phone's own speech service turns speech into text. That service follows its maker's privacy policy, not ours. Typing or keyboard dictation works too.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy. We receive the sale details Gumroad provides to sellers. We use them only to provide your purchase and support.

Hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy.

Files you share

Exported plan files contain what you put in them. Whoever you send a file to can read it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Questions
